# D6 — Metrics

One North Star, four guardrails, and one trust-failure diagnostic. Thresholds are initial operating hypotheses. No baseline exists. Do not manage on certificates issued or the pass rate. Hiring performance is not a metric here.

| Class | Metric | Definition and calculation | Source and cadence | Hypothesis and action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| North Star | Trusted Distinction Rate | Share of surveyed partners who say verification made course completion distinct from demonstrated competency. Numerator: valid answers that the distinction was clear. Denominator: all valid answers to that question from eligible lookups in the period. Track response rate (valid answers ÷ eligible lookups) separately. Lookup volume is context, not the denominator. | Lookups are `CertificateVerificationEvent` rows. The survey is not built. Weekly once it exists. | Below 70% for two consecutive weeks: inspect the verification page, the evidence, the wording, and employer understanding, then test a targeted change. |
| Guardrail | Reviewer Disagreement | Share of final decisions that change the demo proposal. Material changes ÷ decisions. A return is not a material change. | `ReviewerDecision.materialChange`. Weekly. | Above 25%: the heuristic is not a usable proposal. Fix the rubric before adding courses. Do not auto-issue to lower the rate. |
| Guardrail | Median Queue Age | Median from `submittedAt` to the latest decision, for attempts decided that week, plus the oldest Pending Review item. | Attempt and decision timestamps. Count business days at review. Twice a week. | Median above five business days, or any pending item older than ten: pause new assessment launches until capacity or scope changes. |
| Guardrail | Abandonment | Attempts still In Progress after the deadline, or started and not submitted, ÷ starts in the period. | Attempt state, `startedAt`, and `deadlineAt`. Weekly. | Above 40%: inspect time limits and task length before changing the pass rule. |
| Guardrail | Overturn After Issue | Revoked ÷ issued that period. Revocation is the only reversal stored. | Certificate `status`, `issuedAt`, and `revokedAt`. Monthly. | Above 5%: halt issuance for that assessment version and review the rubric. |
| Trust-failure diagnostic | Completion Mistaken for Competency | Count, not a rate: completion-record lookups plus support notes that a completion was offered as skill proof. | Lookups with outcome `completion_record` are stored. Support notes are not. Weekly. | More than ten cases in one week: change employer instructions and the learner-facing wording. Do not relabel those records as certificates. |

Still unbuilt: the distinction survey, a support note for “completion offered as skill proof,” and a routine that computes these figures. The four guardrails use records already kept.
