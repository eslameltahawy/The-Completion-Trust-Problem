# D9 — Unasked Question

What happens when an employer treats a missing proof 180 Certificate as evidence that the learner is weak?

The brief asks how to distinguish completion from competency. It does not ask how a hiring process will read the absence of the new certificate. If partners use "no verified certificate" as a negative screen, learners who only finished a course, who are waiting in Pending Review, or who decline the assessment pay a penalty the product never intended. That can change adoption, fairness, and whether Career 180 should show competency status to employers at all.

First action: in the partner interviews in D3, ask what they would do with a candidate who has a completion record and no proof 180 Certificate, and what they would do with Not Verified. If absence is used against the learner, change the employer experience before expanding issuance. Do not add a public "not certified" badge.
